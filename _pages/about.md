## Research Interests

I work on AI agents and machine learning systems. My current interests include:

- **Trustworthy and verifiable agents:** Developing principled methods to evaluate, understand, and verify the behavior of AI agents, so that their outputs and decisions can be reliably trusted in complex, real-world tasks.
- **GPU programming and ML systems:** Designing programming systems and infrastructure that make AI workloads efficient and scalable, with an interest in how software and hardware can be co-designed to better support emerging models.
